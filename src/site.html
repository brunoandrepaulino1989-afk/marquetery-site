Furniture as inlaid art. Hundreds of slivers of wood, cut by hand and set into one surface.

Marquetery brings collectable marquetry furniture to homes, hotels and private residences across the United Kingdom.

Each piece is built to order by master craftsmen in Portugal, using techniques that date back to the cabinet-makers of the 17th century, and drawn with the geometry of Art Deco, Cubism and the Memphis group.

Our bespoke service

From veneer to finished piece

10–12 weeks, made to order
I

Selecting the veneer

Sheets of bird's-eye maple, walnut root and wenge are chosen for figure and tone, then matched across the whole piece.

II

Cutting and assembling

Each shape is cut by hand, fitted edge to edge like a puzzle and pressed onto a stable core.

III

Lacquering and finishing

Surfaces are sanded, sealed and polished to a soft satin or a deep gloss lacquer, with brass or gold steel details where the design calls for them.
