Our bespoke service

For interior designers and architects, bespoke is where we do our best work. A dining table for a room no standard size will fit, a cabinet in your client's own palette, a piece that so far exists only as a sketch: we make it.

Bring us a drawing, a mood board or a single photograph. Size, shape, veneer, inlay, lacquer and metalwork are all open to you. If it can be drawn, our craftsmen in Portugal can cut it in wood, so there is no brief too ambitious to send us.

From veneer to finished piece

10–12 weeks, made to order
I

Selecting the veneer

Sheets of bird's-eye maple, walnut root and wenge are chosen for figure and tone, then matched across the whole piece.

II

Cutting and assembling

Each shape is cut by hand, fitted edge to edge like a puzzle and pressed onto a stable core.

III

Lacquering and finishing

Surfaces are sanded, sealed and polished to a soft satin or a deep gloss lacquer, with brass or gold steel details where the design calls for them.
