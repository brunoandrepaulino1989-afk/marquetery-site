About Marquetery.uk

Our Story

Marquetry, made for British rooms

Marquetery.uk began with a simple idea: to bring contemporary marquetry, the art of laying veneers into pictures and patterns, into British homes. The company was founded in London in 2024 to represent the work of HOMMES Studio, a Portuguese design studio whose furniture is cut, inlaid and finished by hand.

We hold the exclusive right to market HOMMES designs in the United Kingdom, and a non-exclusive right to offer them beyond it. Every piece in our collection carries a Marquetery name and is made to order for the room it is going to.

2024 · London

A London company

Marquetery.uk is the trading name of After Hours Collections Ltd, incorporated in London in March 2024. From our base in the capital we look after clients, designers and architects across the country.

The designs

The work of HOMMES Studio

Our collection is designed by HOMMES Studio in Portugal: tables, cabinets, mirrors and totems built around bold graphic veneers, burr woods and lacquer. We give each design a Marquetery name of its own.

The United Kingdom

Exclusive rights in the UK

Marquetery.uk acquired from HOMMES the exclusive right to market and sell its designs in the United Kingdom. Here, these pieces are offered only through us.

Beyond the UK

Clients further afield

Our agreement with HOMMES also gives us a non-exclusive right to sell the designs outside the United Kingdom, so we can make and deliver pieces for clients and projects abroad.

The workshop

Made to order in Portugal

Nothing is made for stock. Each piece is cut, laid and lacquered by hand once it is ordered, so sizes, veneers and finishes can be chosen to suit the room.

Delivery

White glove, by Gander & White

All of our logistics and every delivery are handled by Gander & White, specialists in moving fine art and antiques. Each piece travels to its new home with free white glove delivery anywhere in the United Kingdom: carried in, unwrapped, placed and checked.
